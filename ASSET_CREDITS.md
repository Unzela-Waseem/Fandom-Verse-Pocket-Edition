# Bundled media credits

The Explore media bundle is designed for reliable offline playback:

- `assets/media/creator_focus_bee.mp4` — CC0 video, originally from Pixabay, distributed through the [Flutter assets-for-api-docs repository](https://github.com/flutter/assets-for-api-docs).
- `assets/media/creator_focus_butterfly.mp4` — CC0 video, originally from Pixabay, distributed through the [Flutter assets-for-api-docs repository](https://github.com/flutter/assets-for-api-docs).

They are neutral demo visuals. They must not be presented as footage from a particular fandom, artist, event, or licensed franchise. Replace them through the Admin content workflow when approved production media is available.

The `.webp` cover artwork in `assets/media/` was generated as original, franchise-neutral Fandom Verse artwork. The two MP3 tracks (`cosmic_atmosphere.mp3` and `arcade_pulse.mp3`) are original synthetic audio created for this app's offline Audio Room demo.
